Smart Object Explorer

Smart Object Explorer is an AI-powered campus biodiversity system designed to help visitors identify and learn about birds, insects, reptiles, plants, and other living organisms found within the Agastya campus.

How it works:

  • Open the application and fill in your Name, Occupation, and Reason for visit
  • Click “Proceed to Explorer” to start
  • allow camera permissions
  • Point the camera toward:
  • 🐦 Birds
  • 🦋 Insects
  • 🐍 Reptiles
  • 🌿 Plants or flowers
  • Click the 📷 Capture button
  • 📍 Learn about biodiversity within the campus environment
